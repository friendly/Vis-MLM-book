# AI use
Here’s a brief summary:

•	I wrote the entire draft of the manuscript myself. Wrote the code to generate all R figures, … I did ask AI for an opinion on what to include in my chapter summaries.

•	When a consistent change had to be made across all chapters in the book, I used Claude to identify these fix them.

•	There were many, many technical details involved with writing for PDF & HTML, for which Gavin & I engaged AI agents to avoid problems we were dealing with, just to “compile” the book in both formats, and take account of the subject & author indices.

•	Also, for the use by others writing R/Quarto books for CRC Press, I’ve written a couple of blog posts on some details: https://friendly.github.io/blog/#category=Vis-MLM 

•	We also used AI tools extensively to:
  o	Correct typos, infelicities in math equations, unresolved cross-references, …
  o	Extract the uses of my R scripts for figures into something I could include as an online appendix to the book: https://friendly.github.io/Vis-MLM-book/30-Rcode.html 
o	Develop & keep track of a plan to obtain permissions for the book

Martin Handford, "The Deep-Sea Divers," a double-page illustration from _Where's Wally? The Fantastic Journey_ (Walker Books), published in the US as _The Great Waldo Search_ (Boston: Little, Brown, 1989; unpaginated) and now as _Where's Waldo? The Fantastic Journey_ (Candlewick Press).

We would reproduce part of this illustration (roughly the left two-thirds of the spread), together with an enlarged inset of the area where Waldo appears, with Waldo circled. If you prefer, we would be glad to use an image supplied by you instead.

to be reprinted in:

_Visualizing Multivariate Data and Models in R_ by Michael Friendly (CRC Press); proposed date of publication is 2027. Approximately 520 pages.

The illustration appears once, as Figure 9.2 in Chapter 9, which deals with collinearity in regression. It supports an analogy: just as Waldo is hard to spot among the many other figures wearing red and white stripes, the important values in a table of statistical diagnostics are easy to miss among many irrelevant ones. The figure takes up less than half of one page (under 0.1% of the book).
